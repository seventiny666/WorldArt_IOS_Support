❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many artworks are included in WorldArt?
WorldArt includes 620+ famous paintings from 56 artists worldwide, covering both Eastern and Western art history.
Q: Are all paintings free to view?
Yes! All artworks are from Wikimedia Commons (public domain). No additional purchases required.
Q: Does the app work offline?
Yes! WorldArt works completely offline. All paintings and data are stored locally on your device.
Q: Can I save my favorite paintings?
Yes! You can favorite any artwork. Your favorites are stored locally and will remain on your device.
Q: Does the app collect my personal data?
No! WorldArt is completely offline and privacy-focused. We don't collect any personal information. Please see our Privacy Policy for details.
Q: How do I change the daily notification time?
Go to Settings in the app, then adjust your preferred notification time. Make sure notifications are enabled in your iOS Settings.
Q: Is there a dark mode?
Yes! WorldArt currently uses a warm, elegant light theme inspired by art museums.
