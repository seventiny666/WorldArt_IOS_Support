❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many artworks and artists are included in WorldArt?
WorldArt includes 755 famous paintings from 60 renowned artists worldwide, covering Eastern and Western art history across multiple eras and movements.
Q: Are all paintings free to view?
Yes! All artworks are from Wikimedia Commons (public domain) or authorized open-source resources. No additional purchases required — WorldArt is completely free with no ads and no in-app purchases.
Q: Does the app work offline?
Yes! WorldArt works completely offline. All paintings, artist data, and artwork details are stored locally on your device. An internet connection is never required for core features.
Q: Can I save my favorite paintings?
Yes! Tap the ❤️ button on any artwork to add it to your favorites. Your favorites are stored locally and will remain on your device even without an internet connection.
Q: Does the app collect my personal data?
No! WorldArt is completely offline and privacy-focused. We don't collect any personal information, no usage analytics, no tracking, and no ads. Please see our Privacy Policy for full details.
Q: What languages are supported?
WorldArt supports 9 languages: Simplified Chinese (简体中文), Traditional Chinese (繁體中文), English, Japanese (日本語), German (Deutsch), French (Français), Spanish (Español), Korean (한국어), and Italian (Italiano).
Q: How do I change the daily notification time?
Go to Settings in the app, then adjust your preferred notification time under "Daily Reminder." Make sure notifications are enabled for WorldArt in your iOS Settings → Notifications.
Q: Is there a dark mode?
Yes! WorldArt supports system dark mode and also has a warm, elegant light theme inspired by art museums. The app automatically adapts to your iOS appearance settings.
Q: Can I listen to artwork descriptions read aloud?
Yes! WorldArt features a built-in text-to-speech (TTS) function. On any artwork detail page, tap the speaker icon 🎧 to have the story and description read aloud in your preferred language.
Q: How does the map feature work?
The Artist Map shows the geographic locations of all featured artists around the world. Tap any pin to see the artist's card and navigate to their works. The map uses Apple MapKit and requires no account or additional permissions.
Q: What is the minimum iOS version required?
WorldArt requires iOS 17.0 or later.
Q: Does this app have in-app purchases or subscriptions?
No! WorldArt is 100% free. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no ads, and no premium features locked behind a paywall. Everything is included.
