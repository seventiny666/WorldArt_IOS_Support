🖼️ WorldArt Privacy Policy

Completely Offline & Privacy-Focused

🌟 WorldArt is completely offline and privacy-focused.
We don't collect any personal information, don't track user behavior, and all data is stored locally on your device only.

1Information We Collect

We collect NO personal information. WorldArt operates completely offline and does not require any personal data to function.

  • ❌ No personal information
  • ❌ No usage analytics
  • ❌ No device identifiers
  • ❌ No location data
  • ❌ No contact information

2How We Use Information

Since we don't collect any information, we don't use any information. All data (favorites, settings, preferences) is stored locally on your device.

3Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using iOS's secure storage mechanisms:

  • ✅ User preferences stored in iOS UserDefaults
  • ✅ Favorites stored locally
  • ✅ No data sent to external servers
  • ✅ Data is deleted when you uninstall the app

4Third-Party Services

WorldArt uses minimal third-party services:

  • Apple App Store: For app distribution
  • Apple StoreKit: For in-app purchases (handled by Apple)

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services. All artwork images are from Wikimedia Commons (public domain).

5Children's Privacy

WorldArt is safe for children of all ages. We don't collect any information from users of any age, including children under 13.

6Data Security

Since no data leaves your device, your privacy is inherently protected:

  • ✅ No network connections required
  • ✅ No data transmission
  • ✅ All data stays on your device
  • ✅ Protected by iOS security features

7Your Rights

Since we don't collect any data, there's no data to access, modify, or delete. You have complete control over your local data:

  • Delete the app to remove all local data
  • Reset app data through iOS Settings
  • No need to request data deletion from us

8International Users

WorldArt works the same way worldwide - completely offline with no data collection, regardless of your location.

9Privacy Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
